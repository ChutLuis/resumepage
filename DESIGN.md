# Constellation / editorial engineering

- Refined original: page `#0a0a12`, surface `#0e0e16`, hairline `#2a2a40`, input/underline `#5f5c78`, caption `#7c7996`, accents `#a78bfa` and `#c4b5fd`. Flat surfaces, 1px borders, 6px radii on buttons/images/tags and 12px on panels; no shadows, glows or gradients. The primary CTA is a white button with ink text; violet is for links, section labels, small dots and the scene.
- Space Grotesk for display (500; 600 only for the hero headline and wordmark), Inter for everything else, IBM Plex Mono only for dates, times and stack strings (13px, lowercase, never tracked). No serif or italic display. Ten named sizes in `tailwind.config.ts` (`text-display-hero` 84px down to `text-label` 13px); the hero is always the largest type and section titles are 44px. Use a 1200px maximum content grid and generous but purposeful section spacing. Lead with proof: the hero closes on a four-stat strip drawn from real copy.
- Keep copy direct and factual in English and Spanish. Do not invent scarcity, outcomes or availability. Show real project imagery and retain demo and proprietary-work disclosures.
- Aquarius is the sole 3D scene: one fixed full-viewport canvas behind the page, a user-approved exception to “no WebGL background.” A three-free `sceneStore` tracks the desktop hero-column or mobile hero anchor; the matching SVG fallback stays in that anchor through a short crossfade and returns if WebGL is lost. The scene's ticker runs after Lenis in the shared ticker, keeping the constellation locked during smooth scrolling. It plays a one-time draw-in and edge pulses; a document-pixel River follows alternating section-side S-curves, widening and brightening downstream, then narrows into discrete strands and falls as an accelerating sheet onto Contact's top hairline and dims to 35% behind anything tagged `data-scene-calm` (body content wrappers, max 8) so it passes under text rather than across it. From that hairline to the end of the page (behind the footer) a full-bleed particle lake fills the background: a meniscus line bright at the impact and fading before the gutters, a plunge glow, perspective-flattened ripples, horizontal shimmer streaks, twinkling glints, splash, and a reflected Aquarius in the open water, each star smearing into a short downward streak. A calm mask darkens the lake behind the Contact text and footer and a `#contact-reflection` band anchors the reflection; the static SVG fallback stays in `#river-pool`. The only approved model exception is the lazy `src/assets/hebe.glb` statue, decoded with MeshoptDecoder; the fallback remains statue-free. Credit: Bertel Thorvaldsen, Hebe, 1816 (plaster cast KAS 2918), SMK – Statens Museum for Kunst, open.smk.dk/artwork/image/KAS2918, Public Domain Mark; 3D scan by SMK. Mobile alpha is shader-dimmed. Hebe is a luminous point cloud sampled from the model (one medium with the stippled constellation, the dust field, the River and the pool): she assembles from stardust, her base drifts away as dust, and scrolling blows her apart. Soft star halos are allowed only inside this scene; UI surfaces keep no shadows, glows or gradients. Reduced motion uses native scrolling and renders a static settled frame only when scroll, resize, layout or ready state is dirty.
- Reflow to a single column on small screens; avoid clipping and forced headline breaks. Keep native scrolling, visible keyboard focus, 44px touch targets and accessible navigation. Every section opens with `SectionHead` (hairline draw-in, a sentence-case violet label over the masked title words, and an optional factual note such as "5 projects") directly under `Section`; its body content follows in one subtle `Reveal` fade-up. The Contact finale leads with a large `mailto` link (smaller than the hero), an Elsewhere list, the live Guatemala time and the form in a bordered panel, closing on the reflected constellation.
