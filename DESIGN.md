# Constellation / editorial engineering

- Refined Aurora v2: page `#0a0a12`, surface `#0e0e16`, hairline `#2a2a40`, accents `#8b5cf6` and `#a78bfa`. Flat surfaces, 1px rules, restrained corners; no shadows, glows or gradients.
- Space Grotesk for display, Inter for body, IBM Plex Mono for labels and Newsreader italic for testimonial quotes and the single hero headline accent. Use a 1200px maximum content grid, asymmetric columns and generous but purposeful section spacing.
- Keep copy direct and factual in English and Spanish. Do not invent scarcity, outcomes or availability. Show real project imagery and retain demo and proprietary-work disclosures.
- Aquarius is the sole 3D scene: one fixed full-viewport canvas behind the page, a user-approved exception to “no WebGL background.” A three-free `sceneStore` tracks the desktop hero-column or mobile hero anchor; the matching SVG fallback stays in that anchor through a short crossfade and returns if WebGL is lost. The scene's ticker runs after Lenis in the shared ticker, keeping the constellation locked during smooth scrolling. It plays a one-time draw-in and edge pulses; a document-pixel River follows alternating section-side S-curves, widening and brightening downstream, then falls as an accelerating sheet onto Contact's top hairline. From that hairline to the end of the page (behind the footer) a full-bleed particle lake fills the background: a meniscus line on the hairline, a plunge glow, travelling ripple rings, slow downstream drift, twinkling glints, splash, and a reflected Aquarius in the open water below the form. A calm mask softens the lake behind the Contact text and footer; the static SVG fallback stays in `#river-pool`. The only approved model exception is the lazy `src/assets/hebe.glb` statue, decoded with MeshoptDecoder; the fallback remains statue-free. Credit: Bertel Thorvaldsen, Hebe, 1816 (plaster cast KAS 2918), SMK – Statens Museum for Kunst, open.smk.dk/artwork/image/KAS2918, Public Domain Mark; 3D scan by SMK. Mobile alpha is shader-dimmed. Hebe is a luminous point cloud sampled from the model (one medium with the stippled constellation, the dust field, the River and the pool): she assembles from stardust, her base drifts away as dust, and scrolling blows her apart. Soft star halos are allowed only inside this scene; UI surfaces keep no shadows, glows or gradients. Reduced motion uses native scrolling and renders a static settled frame only when scroll, resize, layout or ready state is dirty.
- Reflow to a single column on small screens; avoid clipping and forced headline breaks. Keep native scrolling, visible keyboard focus, 44px touch targets, accessible navigation and one subtle reveal per section.
